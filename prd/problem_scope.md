# Problem & Scope

## Problem

Build a multi-tenant e-commerce platform that enables merchants to manage commerce operations securely, reliably, and at scale.

## In Scope

- Merchant and user management
- Customer management
- Product catalog and variants
- Pricing
- Inventory
- Orders
- Payments and refunds
- Shipping and fulfillment
- Tenant isolation
- Audit and history

## Out of scope
- Multi-vendor marketplace
- Accounting and ERP
- Warehouse automation
- Advanced analytics / data warehouse

## Future extensibility

Although the capabilities above are out of scope for the initial version, the system should be designed so they can be introduced in the future without requiring a fundamental redesign of the core architecture.
